# Color

Two themes, light and dark, switched by a `dark` class on `<html>` (the reader's system
setting by default). Every color is a CSS custom property in `globals.css`, reached through a
Tailwind utility (`bg-background`, `text-brand-text`). The swatches below read their values
from that file.

## The brand in use

The brand color appears as itself only where there's no text on it. Text in the brand color
uses `brand-text`, and white labels sit on `brand-fill`.

## Rules of thumb

- Text: `text-foreground`, or `text-muted-foreground` for anything secondary. Not
  `text-primary`.
- Brand-colored text: `text-brand-text`. White on orange: `bg-brand-fill text-white` (the
  Button's `branded` variant, the Badge's `brand` variant).
- Raw palette colors (`bg-zinc-…`, `#…`) don't appear on the site, except white and black
  over photographs: the reading feed, the ticker, the high-impact hero.
- Emails (`src/emails/`) can't use custom properties, and hard-code `#ff401a`.
