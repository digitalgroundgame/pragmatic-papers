# Color

Two themes, light and dark, switched by a `dark` class on `<html>` (the reader's system
setting by default). Every color is a CSS custom property in `globals.css`, reached through a
Tailwind utility (`bg-background`, `text-brand-text`). The swatches below read their values
from that file.

## The brand in use

The brand color appears as itself only where there's no text on it. Text in the brand color
uses `brand-text`, and white labels sit on `brand-fill`.

## Rules of thumb

- Text: `text-foreground`, or `text-muted-foreground` for anything secondary. Not
  `text-primary`, which only the Button's and Badge's `link` variants and a few link hovers
  still use.
- Brand-colored text: `text-brand-text`. White on orange: `bg-brand-fill text-white` (the
  Button's `branded` variant, the Badge's `brand` variant).
- Raw palette colors (`bg-zinc-…`, `#…`) don't appear on the site. White and black do:
  white labels on `brand-fill`, white text over photographs (the reading feed, the
  high-impact hero), and black for the admin bar and the sheet's overlay.
- Emails (`src/emails/`) can't use custom properties, and hard-code `#ff401a`.
- Colors are written in `oklch()`, the tokens and any one-off alike. Hex stays only where
  `oklch()` can't be read: emails, the pictures the server draws (sharp and `next/og`), and
  sign-in buttons that follow a provider's published colors. Those hex copies name their
  token, and `src/design/__tests__/oklch.test.ts` fails when one drifts from it, since nothing
  else would notice.
- Data has one palette: the diverging red/blue `map-*` scale, for maps, charts and the whip
  count alike. There are no separate chart colors.
