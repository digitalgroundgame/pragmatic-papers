# Page audit

Every public page, measured on a seeded local site at 390px and 1280px wide, against the
common pattern: breadcrumbs, then a `mx-auto max-w-3xl space-y-6 px-4` column with a
36 / 48px `h1`. The data is `src/design/pageLayouts.ts`; when you fix a page, update its
entry there (the test checks each page's wrapper against its source).
