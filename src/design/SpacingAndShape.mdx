# Spacing and shape

Spacing is Tailwind's 4px scale (`1` = 0.25rem). There are no spacing tokens of our own; these
are the values the pages repeat.

<Unstyled>
  <SpacingTable />
</Unstyled>

## Where it's inconsistent today

- Section spacing is `space-y-6` on most pages, `space-y-3` on a volume and `space-y-8` on
  the account page.
- A Separator gets `my-6` almost everywhere and `my-16` on a contributor's page.
- Nothing pads a page's bottom: the space above the footer is whatever margin the last
  element brings, from none to 140px.
- Blocks on built pages each choose their own vertical margins.

## Corners

`--radius` is 0.625rem (10px), and Tailwind's radius steps are multiples of it. The site's
default is `rounded-sm` (6px). shadcn's Card is `rounded-xl`, and every card on the site
overrides it to `rounded-sm`.

## Borders and shadows

Every element's border is `border-border` by default, 1px. Images and cards get a `border`;
article and doc hero images add `shadow`. Rules between sections are the Separator
component, or a `border-t` / `border-b` on the section.
