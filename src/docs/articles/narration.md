An article can carry a recording of itself read aloud. Readers get an audio player at the top of the article, beside its dates, and the player credits whoever narrated it.

## 1. Get the script

On the article, open the **Narration** tab. The **Narration Script** panel's **Generate Narration Text** button turns the article into plain text to read from. **Edit Text** lets you tidy it up and **Copy to Clipboard** copies it. The script isn't shown to readers.

## 2. Upload the recording

Narrators and every other staff role can upload to **Media**. When the file is audio, two more fields appear:

- **Narrator**: who recorded it. Only users with the Narrator role are offered.
- **Duration**: filled in from the file for you; it shows _Calculating…_ for a moment.

The narrator you pick is the one the player credits.

## 3. Attach it to the article

On the article's **Narration** tab, choose the recording in **Audio File** (it only offers audio) and save. The player appears once the article is published.

## Who does what

- **Narrators** upload recordings and can edit or delete their own uploads, but can't edit articles. Once it's uploaded, tell the article's writer or an editor.
- **Writers** can attach the recording to their article; an editor publishes it.
- **Editors** can attach it and publish.

A recording on a published article can't be deleted from Media until it's taken off the article (see [Why some media can't be deleted](/docs/media-references)).
