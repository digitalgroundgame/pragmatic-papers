A topic is a subject that runs across articles, like elections or the courts. Tag an article with its topics and readers can follow a subject from one article to the next.

## Tag an article

In an article's sidebar, pick one or more topics in **Topics**. If the one you want doesn't exist yet, you can create it right there from the picker.

## What readers see

- **On the article**, its topics show as links.
- **Each topic has a page** at `/topics/<slug>`, with its description and the articles tagged with it.
- [**/topics**](/topics) lists every topic.
- **Search** finds topics by name, and an article's topics help it rank when someone searches for them.

## Create or edit a topic

Topics live in the admin under **Collections → Topics**. A topic has:

- **Name**: required, and no two topics can share one.
- **Description**: optional, shown on the topic's page.
- **Slug**: the end of its address. It fills in from the name when you leave it blank.
- An **SEO** tab, like an article's, for how its page appears in search and link previews. See [Fill in the SEO tab](/docs/seo-tab).

Topics have no drafts: saving publishes the change straight away.

## Who can do what

Writers can create topics. Only editors and above can rename or delete one, since a change affects every article tagged with it.

Changing a slug changes the topic page's address, and the old address isn't redirected. Avoid renaming slugs of topics that have been shared or linked from the header or footer.
