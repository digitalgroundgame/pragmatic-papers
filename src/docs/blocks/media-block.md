The **Media** block shows a single image or video. For several at once, use [Media Collage](/docs/media-collage).

## Adding one

Insert **Media** (type `/media` in the editor) and choose a file, or upload a new one from the same window. Only images and videos are offered.

## What readers see

- **Photos** sit slightly wider than the text. Clicking one opens it full size, with its caption.
- **Videos** play on their own, muted and looping, with no controls, like a moving picture. Use a Social Embed for a video readers should watch with sound.
- The **caption** shows under the photo.

## Captions and alt text live in Media

A photo's **Alt** text and **Caption** belong to the file in Media, not to the block, so every place it's used shows the same ones. Write alt text for someone who can't see the photo: what's in it and why it matters here.

Need a photo? You can [search Unsplash](/docs/unsplash-photos) from any upload.
