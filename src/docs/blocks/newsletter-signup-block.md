The **Newsletter Signup** block is a small form where readers enter their email and subscribe to the newsletter. Addresses go straight to our mailing list.

Add it to a page's layout, or inside a Content block's text to sit beside other columns. Then set:

- **Heading**: _Newsletter Signup_ unless you change it.
- **Description**: a line on what subscribers get and how often.
- **Button Label**: _Subscribe_ unless you change it.
- **Notice**: small print under the form, such as a link to the privacy policy.

Readers see a confirmation once they're subscribed, or a message to try again if something went wrong.
