Readers earn **achievements**. One with a badge shows beside their name on their comments, and their account page at /account lists every achievement they hold.

## Define achievements

Open **Content → Achievements**. Every site starts with two, which you can rename, describe, reorder or delete like any other:

- **Verified**: Google or Discord verified the reader's email address. It follows the address: a reader who connects Google or Discord gets it, and it goes if the address stops being verified.
- **Early member**: one of the first 1,000 accounts, readers and staff, in the order they were made.

Click **Create new** to add one. Give it a name and a description (the account page shows both), then choose how it's earned in **Earned by**:

- **Given by hand**: a moderator gives it to a reader from their record (see below).
- **First members**: the first accounts made, readers and staff, up to **How many members**. Saving it awards it at once to the accounts that already qualify, in sign-up order. Raising the number later awards it to the next ones; lowering it takes nothing away. It's kept from then on, and deleting an account never moves anyone else up.
- **Verified email**: worked out from the reader's account each time.

**Earned by** can't be changed once the achievement is saved: make a new one instead.

Untick **Show as a badge beside their name** for an achievement that should only appear on the account page. A comment shows one badge. **Staff** comes first; otherwise it's the reader's achievement that's highest in the list. Drag achievements in the list to reorder them.

Deleting an achievement takes it away from everyone who held it. Only admins can delete one.

## Give or revoke one

Open the reader's record in **Users**. **Achievements** lists the ones they were awarded or given.

- **Give one**: click **Add new**, choose an achievement that's **Given by hand**, and save. Achievements that are earned can't be handed out.
- **Revoke one**: open it from the list, tick **Revoked**, write why in **Why it was revoked**, and save.
- **Revoke Verified**: it isn't in the list until it's revoked. Click **Add new**, choose **Verified**, tick **Revoked**, give a reason, and save.

The badge disappears from their comments at once. The row keeps who revoked it and when, as a record. To give one back, open it and untick **Revoked**. Only admins can delete a row.

Achievements are only about the account. Violations and locks on commenting don't touch them.

## Where it's switched on

Achievements are a beta feature. Admins switch them on in **System → Plugins** under **Achievements**. While they're off, no badge shows except Staff, and the account page leaves them out. First-members achievements are still awarded to new accounts while it's off, so the order holds when it's switched on.
