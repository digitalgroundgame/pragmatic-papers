The **Contributors** block lists people with their photo and bio, for a masthead or an "About us" page. It's in the layout of pages.

1. Insert **Contributors**.
2. Give the list a **Title**, such as _Editorial team_.
3. Pick the **People**, in the order they should appear. Drag them to reorder.

Only staff and people with a public profile are offered, because each card links to that person's contributor page. The photo and bio come from each person's account, so update them there.
