A volume is an issue of Pragmatic Papers: a numbered set of articles with an editor's note. Editors, chief editors and admins build them.

## Making one

1. Go to **Volumes** and click **Create New**.
2. The **Volume Number** in the sidebar is already set to the next one. Its URL is that number.
3. On the **Content** tab, write a short **Description** (required) and the **Editor's Note**.
4. Under **Articles**, pick the articles and drag them into the order readers should get them.
5. Publish.

## Good to know

- **Every article has to be published first.** Saving a volume with an unpublished article fails and names the ones still in draft.
- You can't create an article from the volume; write and publish it first, then add it here.
- Volumes autosave and keep versions just like articles, and can be scheduled with **Schedule Publish**.
- **Published At** fills itself in when you first publish, if you left it empty.
