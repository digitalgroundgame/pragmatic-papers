Some features are easier to show than to describe. When a help doc comes with a guided tour, the help bell gives it two buttons: **Show me** starts the tour in the admin, and **Read more** opens the doc. A doc with a tour has the same Show me button at the top.

## Taking a tour

A tour points at one thing at a time and says what it's for. Use **Next** and **Back** to move through it. A tour can change pages, so it may open a list or an article for you on the way. If you click the highlighted thing yourself, the tour follows you to the next page.

End it at any time with the × or Escape. If you go to a page the tour doesn't cover, it ends on its own.

## The first tour

Show me on this doc goes from the dashboard to an article: where articles live, how to start or find one, and then, in the editor, the Content, SEO and Narration tabs, the status, the preview and the publish button.
