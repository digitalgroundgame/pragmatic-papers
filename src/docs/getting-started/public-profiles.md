**What changed:** author pages moved from `/authors` to `/contributors` (old links still work), and the new **Public author page** setting keeps a page for anyone credited on an article, whatever their role. Both are described below.

Each person credited on the site has a contributor page at `/contributors/<your-name>`. Your byline links to it, and [/contributors](/contributors) lists everyone.

## What's on it

- Your **Profile Image**, round. Without one, lists show your initials.
- Your **Name**, **Affiliation** and **Socials**.
- Your **Biography**, under "Bio".
- Your articles.

The link preview when someone shares your page uses your profile image. Your email address never appears.

## Fill yours in

Click your avatar in the top right of the admin to open your account, then fill in:

- **Name**, as it should appear on bylines.
- **Affiliation**: a short line under your name, like a title or organisation.
- **Biography**: a few paragraphs about you. Lists show its first couple of lines, so lead with the important part.
- **Profile Image**: a square photo works best.
- **Socials**: up to 6 links, like your site or accounts.

Save, and your page updates.

**Slug** is the end of your page's address. It's made from your name when your account is created.

## Who has a page

Everyone with a staff role (writer, narrator, editor, chief editor, admin) has a public page. So does anyone an article credits as an author, or a recording credits as its narrator: they get **Public author page** ticked automatically, which keeps their page and bylines working even if their role changes later.

Admins and chief editors can tick or untick **Public author page** by hand, and they can edit anyone's profile. Everyone else can edit only their own.

The [Contributors block](/docs/contributors-block) offers the same people: staff, and anyone with a public page.
