Every article starts as a draft. Readers only see it once it's published, and only editors can publish.

## Writers

- Create articles and edit the ones **you created**, whether they're drafts or already published.
- You can't publish, and you can't change a published article's live version. When a draft is ready, tell an editor.
- You can delete articles you created.

## Editors

Editors, chief editors and admins can edit, publish, schedule and delete any article. They also have two sidebar fields writers can't change:

- **Published At**: the date shown on the article. Left empty, it's set to the moment the article is first published.
- **Syndicate to Substack**: adds the article to the feed Substack imports from, once it's published.

## Saving and versions

Changes save themselves as a draft while you type, so there's no Save button to forget. The last 50 versions are kept; the **Versions** tab lets you compare them or restore one.

## Live Preview

**Live Preview** in the top bar shows the draft beside the editor, laid out as readers will see it and updating as you type. **Preview** opens the same view in its own tab.

## Scheduling

To publish later, an editor opens the menu beside **Publish changes** and picks **Schedule Publish**, then a date and time. The article goes live on its own at that time.

## Around the article

- **Content**, **SEO** and **Narration** are tabs at the top. See [Add a narration to an article](/docs/narration) for the last one.
- The sidebar holds the hero image, slug, authors and topics. **Authors** offers writers, editors, chief editors and narrators.
