The **Collection Grid** block is how pages like the home page feature articles and volumes in a designed arrangement. It's in the layout of pages.

## Setting it up

1. Insert **Collection Grid**.
2. Choose a **Layout Preset**. The preset decides how many slots there are and how they're arranged, from a single featured story (_Bernoulli Left_ or _Bernoulli Right_) up to _Gauss 10_, which takes eight to ten.
3. Fill each slot under **Slots** with an **Article or Volume**. Each row is labelled with its place in the layout, such as _Left Column_. Drag rows to swap stories between places.

Only published articles and volumes are offered, newest first.

## Per slot

- **Kicker**: a short label above the title, such as _Breaking_ or _Opinion_.
- **Show Byline**: shows the authors' names.
- **Override Title**: a different title for this spot only; the article keeps its own.

## Changing the preset

Picking a different preset adds or removes slots to match, so the number of rows is always right. Switching to a smaller preset drops the slots at the end, so move the stories you want to keep up first. Saving checks the count too, and says how many the preset needs if it's off.
