The **Merch** block shows a carousel of products from the Shopify store, each linking to the store, with a **Shop all** button.

## Where products come from

Products live under **Store → Merch** and copy themselves from Shopify every six hours. To pick up a change sooner, click **Sync Shopify** above the list. You can't create a product here, and prices, titles and stock are read-only: change those in Shopify. A product Shopify stops listing is archived and never shown.

Each product has a **Presentation** section that's ours and survives every sync:

- **Featured**: for blocks set to show featured products only.
- **Hidden**: keeps it out of every Merch block without touching Shopify.
- **Badge Override**: a short label such as _Last few_, up to 20 characters. Sold-out products get _Sold Out_ on their own.
- **Sort Order**: lower numbers come first.

## Adding the block

The block is available on pages, in the page layout or inside a Content block. Choose:

- **Layout**: _Full width (page)_ or _Square (sidebar)_.
- **Source**: _All synced products_, or _Filtered selection_ to narrow by Shopify collection (for example `apparel`), tag, featured products, or products you pick by hand. Hand-picked products appear in the order you arrange them.
- **Order By**: _Sort order_, _Title (A–Z)_ or _Newest first_.
- **Limit**: up to 24 products; 12 by default.
