Readers who comment earn **achievements**. Each one shows as a badge beside their name on their comments, and their account page at /account lists every achievement they hold.

## The achievements

- **Verified**: Google or Discord verified the reader's email address. It follows the address: a reader who connects Google or Discord gets it, and it goes if the address stops being verified.
- **Early member**: one of the first 1,000 readers to make an account, in the order they signed up. It's awarded when the account is made and kept from then on. Deleting an account never moves anyone else up, and staff accounts don't count.

A comment shows one badge. **Staff** comes first; otherwise the reader's highest achievement shows, Early member before Verified.

Achievements are only about the account. Violations and locks on commenting don't touch them.

## Revoke one

Open **Content → Achievements**.

- **Early member**: find the reader's row, tick **Revoked**, write why in **Why it was revoked**, and save.
- **Verified** has no row until it's revoked. Click **Create new**, choose the reader and **Verified**, tick **Revoked**, give a reason, and save.

The badge disappears from their comments at once. The row keeps who revoked it and when, as a record.

To give one back, open the row and untick **Revoked**. Achievements can't be handed out in the admin: a row made there only ever revokes. Only admins can delete a row.

## Where it's switched on

Achievements are a beta feature. Admins switch them on in **System → Plugins** under **Achievements**. While they're off, no badge shows except Staff, and the account page leaves them out. Early member is still awarded to new accounts while it's off, so the order holds when it's switched on.
