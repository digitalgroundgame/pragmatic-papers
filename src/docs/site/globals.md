Some things appear on every page rather than in one document: the header's menu, the footer, the ticker. Each has a single settings page in the admin's sidebar, under **Globals** or **System**. Saving one changes the whole site, usually within a minute.

## Header

Admins only. **Header** holds:

- **Navigation Items**: the menu, up to 12 links.
- **Action Buttons**: up to 4 buttons in the top right. Give the most important one the **Branded** appearance.

Each link either points at a page, volume, article or topic on the site (**Internal link**), which keeps working if its address changes, or at any address (**Custom URL**). **Open in new tab** is for links that leave the site.

## Footer

Admins only. **Footer** holds:

- **Layout**: text blocks shown above the links.
- **Navigation Items**: up to 6 links.
- **Social Links**: up to 10 links to our accounts.
- **Copyright**: the line at the bottom. The © and the current year are added for you, so write only the name.

## Ticker

Editors and admins. The ticker is the strip under the header that shows a live broadcast and our latest posts. **Ticker → Hidden posts** lists posts it leaves out: paste a post's link from Bluesky or X, as its Share button copies it, and save. It's gone from the ticker straight away.

## System settings

Admins only, under **System**:

- **Site Settings** switches beta features on or off for this site. See [Switch beta features on per site in Site Settings](/docs/experiments).
- **Integrations** chooses whose accounts the ticker shows: up to 5 YouTube channels (when two are live at once, the first listed wins), Bluesky accounts and X accounts. A table at the top shows which outside services are connected. Keys and passwords are never entered here; they're set where the site is hosted.
- **Article Recommendations** shows the articles ranked by readers' engagement. It updates itself; there's nothing to edit.

## Staging and the live site

Staging and the live site each keep their own copy of these settings. A change made on staging doesn't reach the live site, so make it in both if you want it in both.
