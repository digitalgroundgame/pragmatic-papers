Articles, pages, volumes, topics and interactive pages each have an **SEO** tab next to **Content**. What you put there is what people see before they click: the title and description in a Google result, and the card that appears when someone shares the link on social media or in a message.

## The fields

- **Overview** checks the tab for you, for example _3/3 checks are passing_, and flags each field as **Good**, **Missing**, **Too short** or **Too long**.
- **Title**: the headline in search results and on the browser tab. Aim for 50 to 60 characters; the counter tells you how many to go. **Auto-generate** fills in the document's title followed by "| The Pragmatic Papers".
- **Meta Image**: the picture on the link's card. Keep it under 500 KB. It's cropped to a wide 1200 × 630, so keep the subject near the middle.
- **Description**: a sentence or two under the title in search results and on the card. Aim for 100 to 150 characters.
- **Preview** shows roughly how the result will look in Google.

## Write the description yourself

On an article, **Auto-generate** only fills in the site's general description, so write your own: say what the article finds or argues, not just its subject. It's also the excerpt readers see in site search, in recommended articles and on Collection Grid cards.

On volumes and topics, **Auto-generate** copies their own Description field.

## When you leave a field empty

- **An article's Meta Image** is filled from its hero image when you save.
- **A volume's Title** becomes "Volume" and its number, like _Volume XII | The Pragmatic Papers_.
- Anything else left empty falls back to the site's name, general description and logo card, which makes every link look the same. Fill the tab in for anything you expect to be shared.

## Tips

- Lead with the words people would search for.
- Don't repeat the title in the description; add to it.
- Check the card before a big share: paste the link into a message to yourself.
