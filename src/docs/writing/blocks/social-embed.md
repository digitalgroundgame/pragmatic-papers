The **Social Embed** block shows a post, video or thread from another platform right in the article, the way that platform displays it.

## Adding one

1. Insert **Social Embed** (type `/social` in the editor).
2. Paste the post's link into **URL**. A green tick means we recognise it; a red cross means the link isn't from a platform we support or isn't a post.

Supported: **X (Twitter)**, **YouTube**, **Reddit**, **Bluesky** and **TikTok**. Copy the link from the post itself (its share button or the address bar), not from a profile or search page.

For X posts, two extra boxes appear: **Hide Media** leaves out the post's photos or video, and **Hide Thread** shows just that post without the one it replied to.

## If the post is deleted later

When you save or publish, we fetch the post and keep a copy with the article. While the post is live, readers get the platform's own interactive version. We check back about once a month; if the post has since been deleted or made private, readers see our saved copy instead of a blank space.

If we couldn't fetch the post at all when you saved (a typo, a private account, the platform was down), readers get a small notice with a link to view it on the platform. Check the preview after publishing, and save again once the link works.

## Good to know

- The copy is taken when you save, not while you type, so the preview may show the post only after you save.
- Embeds load the platform's own script, which can be slow. One or two per article is plenty.
