Math is written in LaTeX. There are two blocks:

- **Inline Math Block** sits inside a sentence, like a word.
- **Display Math Block** goes on its own line, centred, for longer equations. It's in the block menu with the other blocks.

Fill in **Math Expression** with the LaTeX. Use **Description** to say what the formula is in plain words: screen readers and the narration voice read it instead of the symbols.
