## Overview

Each issue is assigned story points. We try to assign points and size to each issue to give you a sense of the scope of the issue.

## Fibonacci-inspired Story Points

- 0.5,1: **XS** Very simple; updates or small fixes.
- 2,3: **SM-MD** Small to medium tasks; routine development.
- 5: **L** Large; significant effort.
- 8,13: **XL** Extra-large; approaches the limit of a sprint and often requires breaking down.
- 20+: Generally considered too large and must be split into sub-issues.
